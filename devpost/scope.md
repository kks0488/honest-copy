# Honest Copy
A small product-copy checker that explains risky phrases and helps sellers write more carefully.

## The Unique Kernel
Rules first, phrase-level explanations and safer rewrites for English and Korean, with a visible before/after risk indicator. It always works without an AI key.

## Who It's For
User answer: “small online sellers in Korea and abroad who write their own product listings, often late at night.” They currently guess which claims might cause rejection or need evidence.

## The Core Loop
User answer: “paste product copy -> Honest Copy highlights risky phrases inline (color by risk type: health/medical claim, absolute guarantee, unverifiable superlative, missing evidence), explains why in plain words, and offers a safer rewrite for each, with a before/after score.” Review, apply suggestions, copy the revised draft.

## Inspiration & Identity
User asks for English UI, a tiny scope and polished UX. Agent assumption: calm editorial workspace, warm paper, dark green actions and four labeled risk colors; no third-party imagery.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
User answer: “planning before building with an AI agent, keeping decisions in docs so the AI doesn't forget.”

## What "Working" Looks Like
Paste an original fictional listing, see exact highlights and four explanations, apply changes, see the score decrease, copy the revised text, repeat with Korean. A screen recording proves the loop without reviewers running code.

## The POC Boundary
One page; deterministic explainable rules; English/Korean examples; individual and all-template rewrites; score; copy/undo; optional server-only OpenAI-compatible rewriting. No legal or marketplace approval verdicts.

## Later
Larger reviewed rule sets and category-specific evidence workflows after validating usefulness.

## Explicitly Cut
Accounts, databases, payments and scraping: expressly excluded. Platform integrations and legal determinations: unnecessary for the phrase-level proof. No paid APIs used during this build.

## Authorization and Assumptions
Interview answers come from the user's brief, quoted above. User explicitly directs autonomous reasonable assumptions, no questions, planning before code, publishing and deployment. Planning approval is delegated implementation authorization, not a claim of interactive learner review.
